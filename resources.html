PROJECT ARES 3D / SOURCES AND OBJECTIVES

Practice mission decisions

The Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer challenge proposes a game for students aged 7–12 to manage a Moon or Mars outpost and experience the consequences of their decisions.

Challenge objectives included

  • Life support: restoring air uses power.
  • Radiation: protecting the crew limits activities and costs resources.
  • Power: allocate it among essential systems, communications, crops and science.
  • Food: maintaining crops uses power.
  • Communication delay: the crew makes local decisions while awaiting Earth’s reply.

These concepts appear in five playable sols at a Mars outpost, simulated from the spacecraft cabin. Each decision shows its costs and consequences. The previous six scenarios are Earth-based training.

Simulation scope. Resource percentages, thresholds and events are fictional educational mechanics. No game value is presented as NASA telemetry or live data. The links below provide further information. The demo is fictional and not affiliated with NASA.
