# Project Ares glove model

Adapted from the "Astronaut in Spacesuit" light model by Grab3D: https://grab3d.com/models/characters/astronaut-in-spacesuit/ (CC0 1.0; see LICENSE.txt). Grab3D discloses that its source model and textures were AI-assisted and cleaned up in Blender. The glove geometry and the original embedded material textures were extracted and transformed for first-person use. The cropped model is static; Project Ares derives a simple shared fingertip bend at runtime. It is a fictional design and is not a certified or NASA-provided glove.

The previously supplied NASA Astronaut Glove GLB (DigitalSpace Corporation) informed the design, but is not incorporated in this asset. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/astronaut-glove/.
