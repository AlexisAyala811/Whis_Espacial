# Exercise equipment assets

- `bike.glb`: Indoor spin bike with front flywheel, Innerscene, CC0 1.0. https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/indoor-spin-bike-with-front-flywheel-2bb33e74
- `treadmill.glb`: Treadmill, Grab3D, CC0 1.0. https://grab3d.com/models/sports/treadmill/

Both are scaled for the fictional simulation. The treadmill base-color map is desaturated at runtime; the original files remain intact. These are recreational training representations, not exact NASA equipment. Procedural models remain available on low graphics or if a file fails to load.
- `keyboard.glb`: Full-size keyboard, 3DAssets.dev, CC0 1.0 (AI-assisted asset). https://3dassets.dev/assets/computers-and-desk-gadgets-keyboard-fullsize-096b61a4
