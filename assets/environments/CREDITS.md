# NASA imagery used in the scene reference displays

The images appear as labeled archive/reference displays in a fictional simulation. NASA does not endorse or sponsor Project Ares 3D.

- `suit.jpg` — NASA, spacesuit reference; https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-space-suits/ (STS-69 image STS069-714-046).
- `exercise.jpg` — NASA, astronaut training on the T2 treadmill; https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/exercising-t2-treadmill/.
- `control.jpg` — NASA, historic Mission Control flight control room, image 41g-90128; https://images.nasa.gov/details/41g-90128.
- `eva.jpg` — NASA, Neutral Buoyancy Laboratory training; https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/spacewalk-training-neutral-buoyancy-laboratory/.
- `habitat.jpg` — NASA, Human Exploration Research Analog; https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/hera-crew-member-close-quarters/.
- `cabin.jpg` — NASA, Orion crew module; https://www.nasa.gov/gallery/inside-orion/ (image jsc2022e044973).
- The Artemis I vehicle image in the assembly building is credited separately in `assets/rocket/CREDITS.md`.
- NASA image and media guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/.

The archive images are not views through real windows or live mission footage. They complement original 3D backgrounds and the existing Mars and Earth imagery.
